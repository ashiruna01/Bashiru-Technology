About Me

🎓

My name is Ashiruna Bashiru Lutachuzibwa, a teacher and web application developer currently pursuing a Bachelor of Education in Science with ICT (Chemistry & ICT) at the University of Dodoma (UDOM).

💻

I am deeply passionate about the integration of technology into education and everyday life. Beyond teaching, I develop web and management systems that address real community challenges.
